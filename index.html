About Rabbi Yossi

A steady hand for a deeply meaningful day.

Rabbi Yossi Shuchat is a Certified Mohel trained by expert Mohels in New York, Israel and London. He received certification from the internationally renowned London School of Circumcision.

He is a Member of the American Board of Ritual Circumcision and the International Bris Association, and is affiliated with Sunrise Hospital and Sunrise Children's Hospital in Las Vegas.

Known for his warm and caring personality, Rabbi Shuchat is committed to providing confidence and calm to first-time parents. He performs the circumcision in a quick, natural way with minimal pain, and provides proactive follow-up calls and check-ins before and after every procedure. Rabbi Yossi is fluent in English, Hebrew, Spanish and French.
